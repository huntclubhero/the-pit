## SURVIVOR (MED-HIGH, HIGH conf): the at-open instantaneous-deviation proxy does NOT price the PATIENT / seasoned-market straddle harvest (the core A2 threat).
Both Part-2 levers (vol surcharge PerpEngine :564-611, cap discount _applyVolCapDiscount :627-636) read spot-vs-TWAP deviation AT THE OPEN BLOCK only. At that calm open: surcharge = kVol*0 + 0 ~= 0 and cap discount = kCapVol*0 = 0 (full capacity). The cap discount is OPEN-path only, so it can never claw back an already-reserved position riding the vol event. So the wave prices (a) fresh markets (25 bps decaying premium + 1%/14d ramp) and (b) opening DURING elevated deviation, but does NOT touch the patient seasoned-market harvest that A2 called the threat ("open before a known catalyst"). The PoC proves the kill only because it opens at 50 bps deviation on a DAY-ONE market (fresh premium does most of the work); it never exercises a ~0-bps calm open on a seasoned market: that case survives, bounded only by the reserve caps (~2.5% TVL/sybil).
Spot-vs-TWAP deviation measures divergence NOW, not realized vol over the hold. Durable fix (Hunt decision): price convexity as a HOLDING cost, not an OPEN cost: vol-scaled FUNDING/borrow (charge for holding gamma while realized vol accrues), or a short-TWAP-vs-long-TWAP realized-vol spread. A minimum hold does NOT help (the patient harvester holds through the event anyway).
