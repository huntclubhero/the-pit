## FINDING 1 (HIGH, HIGH conf): THE VAULT HAS NO HOUSE EDGE. Trading fees AND liquidation penalties both routed AWAY from the LP; GMX price-impact stripped. The "house" is a zero-edge short-vol counterparty living on funding+borrow alone.
- Execution at oracle mark with ZERO spread + ZERO price impact (_openCore uses ctx.mark directly, fee=openFeeBps only :436-441). GMX v2 (the cited model) charges price-impact on imbalancing trades to compensate the pool for adverse selection. THE PIT drops it.
- 100% of 5/10 bps open+close fees -> jackpot/referral/buyback/treasury, NOTHING to the vault (_distributeFee :1046-1057, called :469/606). GLP earns 100% of trading fees; HLP earns a share. THE PIT vault earns 0%.
- Liquidation penalty 80% IF / 20% keeper / 0% vault; residual equity RETURNED to the liquidated trader (:928-932, :894-896). Hyperliquid routes liquidation PnL to HLP; THE PIT routes it away.
- Vault's ONLY trade income = funding residual + borrow + 10 bps deposit/withdraw churn. totalAssets() (:257-262) has no fee-income term.
Frame: against a zero-spread oracle mark a NOISE trader's EV vs the vault is ~0 (open+close at fair value; not a casino edge, not an AMM spread). Vault earns nothing structural from noise; it collects only funding+borrow and eats the full edge of any informed trader. +EV only if funding+borrow > informed extraction, with NO fee cushion (GLP/HLP both have that cushion; THE PIT deleted it). Magnitude: borrow ~12 bps/day, funding up to 6%/day on net skew, both meaningful only for HELD positions; fast informed/event/latency flow (minutes-hours) -> funding+borrow ~0 -> vault eats informed edge raw. Memecoin flow skews to exactly that toxic kind.
Mismatch: risk-register row 6 says "raise fees/caps if negative" but fees do NOT reach the vault. The only levers that help the LP are funding/borrow coefficients or a vault-captured spread/price-impact. Equilibrium: LP yield = thin funding+borrow + finite $PIT emissions; once emissions taper rational LPs leave, TVL falls, 10%-TVL caps shrink, capacity spirals down = the GLP/HLP toxic-flow death spiral the design claims to solve but WORSENED by removing LP compensation. Tokenomics "real-revenue LP yield, not emission-funded" thesis is NOT supported by the contract.
RECOMMEND (Hunt decision): route a share of trade fee AND/OR the liquidation penalty TO the vault, and/or add a vault-captured spread/price-impact.

## FINDING 2 (HIGH, HIGH conf): CONVEXITY / STRADDLE HARVEST. The vault sells positive convexity at zero premium. A delta-neutral sybil straddle has capped liquidation-truncated downside and 9x upside; any realized move beyond ~1/leverage is profit extracted from the vault. Not in the risk register.
9x payout cap (:86, applied :443) + early liquidation + residual-return = convexity: loss capped small, win up to 9x. Package delta-neutral via two sybils (long+short, same market, same mark). Worked numbers (tier-1 meme, 6x, MMR 10%, cap 9x, fee 10 bps, capital 2M): loser leg liquidated at ~6.7% adverse move, equity ~0.6M, penalty ~0.06M, residual ~0.54M RETURNED -> loser net loss only ~0.46M (the dYdX-style residual-return SHRINKS the loss leg from 1.0M to 0.46M, subsidizing the harvester). Winner leg runs to the 9x cap; at +50%: long +3.0M, short -0.46M, fees -0.02M -> net ~+2.5M on 2M = ~+125% from one 50% move; vault outflow ~2.6M. Flat (|move|<~1/lev): legs offset, cost = fees+borrow ~-2.5%. Break-even move ~1/leverage + fee drag ~17% at 6x; memecoins move 20-50% intraday or open before a known catalyst -> strongly +EV, approaching risk-free. Vault is structurally SHORT GAMMA at zero premium; funding/borrow key to skew/utilization NOT realized vol, so convexity is UNPRICED. Reserve caps BOUND (25%/address, 10%-TVL/market, 2% ramp) but do not prevent: ~2.5% of TVL winning payout/sybil, ~40% of TVL aggregate across 8 markets under 80% util. Repeatable per vol event, NOT in risk register 9. (v1 capped payout symmetrically at 2x = no convexity; v2's 9x + early-liq + residual-return manufactured it.)
RECOMMEND (Hunt decision): price the convexity -> vol-linked open fee credited TO THE VAULT, tighter caps on fresh/high-vol markets, minimum hold, or vol-scaled funding.
