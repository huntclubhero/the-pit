# Perp Audit Wave 1: Funding / Borrow-fee Accrual
Attacker: pa-funding (Fable) | 2026-07-24 | 2 MEDIUM + 1 LOW. Core skew/borrow math CORRECT. Much refuted.

## FINDING A (MEDIUM, conf HIGH): non-LIVE accrual DISCARDS the entire pending funding+borrow interval, not just the non-LIVE slice. Cheap funding-dodge + vault-revenue erosion via permissionless pokeFunding.
PerpEngine _accrue :1332-1349 only books funding/borrow when the print is LIVE; but _aggAccrue :1549-1563 ALWAYS advances lastAccrual to now even on the non-LIVE (zero-delta) branch. So one pokeFunding() call (permissionless) during any FALLBACK/BLOCKED moment zeroes the elapsed dt and the ENTIRE accumulated-but-unbooked funding+borrow for that whole holding interval is forfeited (not just the seconds the oracle was degraded). A long paying funding waits for a routine oracle blip (pool-priced markets degrade often), pokes, and wipes hours of owed funding; symmetrically LP borrow revenue is eroded. FIX (fix-engine): accrue-then-freeze: on a non-LIVE poke, book the LIVE portion up to the state-transition timestamp before advancing lastAccrual (or do not advance lastAccrual across a non-LIVE gap so the interval is billed on the next LIVE poke). Do NOT silently swallow the whole interval.

## FINDING B (MEDIUM -> effectively LOW, conf HIGH mechanism): funding CREDIT is clamped to maxPayout on close/liquidation but UNCLAMPED on modify paths (_settlePending), breaking the "maxPayout bounds every vault outflow" invariant on reduce/removeMargin.
_computeClose clamps winFromVault to pos.maxPayout (:631); _computeLiquidation/_adlCloseVictim clamp (:926,1483); but _reduceCore/_removeMarginCore route the whole-position funding credit through _settlePending into flows.fromVault with NO maxPayout clamp (:655-692, 808-854), bounded only by the vault's market-wide totalReserved in settleTraderWin. Two effects: (1) a position can extract funding-credit value above its own maxPayout via reduce/removeMargin that it could not via close (invariant asymmetry); (2) if the position is the dominant reserve holder and its credit exceeds outstanding totalReserved, the modify reverts WinExceedsReserved (partial DoS; close still works). Reachability is narrow (a lone position is the whole skew so it PAYS funding; a large credit needs an opposing majority that itself inflates totalReserved) -> practical severity LOW, but the close-vs-reduce clamp asymmetry is a real latent inconsistency. FIX: apply the maxPayout clamp consistently across ALL settlement paths.

## FINDING C (LOW/INFO): lazy accrual applies the INSTANTANEOUS rate over the whole elapsed dt in one jump.
Rate computed at poke-time from current skew/utilization and applied to the full interval since lastAccrual (no time-averaging). Between pokes the rate is a step function of the LAST poke's state; a trader can time entry just after an adverse poke. Mitigated: keeper preview + anyone can poke to refresh. Minor: at an extreme stale dt the rate*dt product risks an overflow-revert (brick on that market's accrual until a smaller-dt poke) but dt that large needs a multi-year gap. INFO.

## Refuted (SAFE)
- Delta-neutral funding farm: NET-NEGATIVE (both sides pay borrow; funding is a transfer between sides; the receiver's funding gain <= combined borrow paid). No free carry.
- Same-block skew-flip to dodge/reverse funding: SAFE. _accrue books funding on the PRE-mutation skew BEFORE _applyPositionDelta (accrue-before-mutate ordering verified), so you always pay for the interval you actually held.
- Mark-manip to swing the funding RATE: SAFE. Funding rate is skew-driven (OI imbalance), not mark-driven; the mark enters only via notional scaling which is symmetric; you cannot cheaply move the rate without moving OI (which costs margin + fees).
- Sign/scaling of fundingX1e18 / borrowX1e18 accumulators: CORRECT both sides (longs pay when skew-long, shorts receive; borrow always debits the position, credits vault; entryFundingX/entryBorrowX snapshot at open and delta-charged once per mutation, no double-charge).
